| 10 | 2026-10-13 | Pair Programming with Antigravity | **Topological single-digit 9 classifier and scale-invariant multi-character left digit (1 vs 2) classifier.** Why: In `game6.jpg`, single digit 9 lacked an explicit detection branch and was misclassified as 5 due to upper-left vs upper-right stroke heuristic. Replaced with scale-invariant `leftMaxX` margin check at `yUpper = 0.25 * leftH`, cleanly distinguishing digit '2' (whose top-right curve reaches `leftMaxX`) from digit '1' (whose top curve/stem is centered and far from `leftMaxX`) across all resolutions. | 11 | 2026-10-14 | Pair Programming with Antigravity | **Multi-part board detection, unequal ('≠') constraint recognition, and scale-invariant chevron/digit disambiguation (1 vs 2, 8 vs 9).** Why: In `game7.jpg` and `game8.jpg`, the puzzle contains 3 disconnected sub-boards with an unequal badge '≠'. Game area, grid, cell, and domino detection were extended to support multi-part layouts. In token recognition, '≠' is identified via two horizontal bars with top/bottom-extending diagonal slashes; chevrons ('<', '>') are separated from digits using mid-height apex and open-mouth topology (`midRightPixels === 0` for `<`, `midLeftPixels === 0` for `>`); multi-character '1' vs '2' distinguishes the full vertical column of '1' (`leftMaxCol / leftH > 0.82`) from the wide flat base without continuous vertical stem of '2' (`leftMaxCol / leftH <= 0.82 && leftBotP >= leftW * 0.55 && leftW / leftH >= 0.60`); and single digit '8' vs '9' detects whether the lower-left wall is continuous (loop in '8') or has empty rows (open mouth in '9') across rows `0.60..0.75 * H`. | 12 | 2026-10-15 | Pair Programming with Antigravity | **3-character token parsing (`>14`), antialiasing-tolerant chevron detection, right-digit 2 vs 3 baseline density, and persistent JSON golden cases for all games.** Why: In `game10.jpg`, `>14` was recognized without `>` because 3-character tokens lacked sub-splitting into operator + tens + ones, and antialiased pixels on orange badges were clipped by tight diff thresholds. | 13 | 2026-10-15 | Pair Programming with Antigravity | **Anti-aliasing resilient baseline separation for chevron vs digit 2, wide equal-sign threshold, connected-component noise filtering, and 9 vs 5 upper-right cavity validation.** Why: In browser Canvas rendering, subpixel anti-aliasing caused `24` in Game 1 and `20` in Game 9 to match `isChevronRight` because digit 2's neck mimicked rightward apex pixel counts; wide double-cell equal badge in Game 2 failed `0.55 * glyphW` span check; small 36x36 badge in Game 8 had 3 noise pixels at top-left expanding `minX` and skewing digit 6 left-wall metrics; and Game 1 `<5` triggered 9's stroke rule. Files: `src/importer/tokenRecognition.ts`, `src/importer/badgeDetection.ts`, `golden_results/*.json`. |
